# Career Planner Skill

This skill transforms Codex into an intelligent career planning assistant, helping users make informed decisions about their career development with personalized paths and actionable plans.

## What It Does

- **Career Assessment**: Analyzes user background, skills, and goals
- **Path Planning**: Creates personalized career development roadmaps
- **Skill Gap Analysis**: Identifies skill gaps and learning priorities
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitors career progress over time
- **Goal Setting**: Helps define and achieve career milestones
- **Industry Insights**: Provides industry-specific career advice

## Quick Start

Tell Codex about your career situation:

- "Help me plan my career as a software engineer with 3 years experience"
- "I want to transition from data science to product management"
- "Create a career path for a junior developer"
- "What skills do I need to become a senior engineer?"

Codex will automatically load this skill and provide personalized guidance.

## Career Planning Workflow

### 1. Assessment Phase
- Background review (current role, experience, skills)
- Goal identification (short-term and long-term)
- Industry analysis (trends, demands, opportunities)
- Strength assessment (current capabilities and achievements)

### 2. Planning Phase
- Career path mapping (2-5 year roadmap)
- Skill gap analysis (what to learn and when)
- Milestone creation (specific, measurable goals)
- Resource recommendations (courses, books, mentors)

### 3. Action Phase
- Learning plan prioritization (what to focus on first)
- Timeline establishment (realistic completion dates)
- Progress tracking (regular check-ins and adjustments)
- Opportunity identification (jobs, projects, networking)

### 4. Review Phase
- Milestone achievement assessment
- Plan adjustment based on progress
- Goal refinement and re-prioritization
- Long-term strategy evolution

## Career Path Examples

### Software Engineer Path

**Junior (0-2 years)**:
- Core programming skills
- Version control and collaboration
- Basic testing and debugging
- Project experience

**Mid-level (2-5 years)**:
- System design fundamentals
- Advanced algorithms and data structures
- Framework specialization
- Mentorship skills

**Senior (5+ years)**:
- Architecture and scalability
- Technical leadership
- Business understanding
- Cross-team collaboration

### Data Science to Product Management Path

**Phase 1: Foundation** (0-6 months)
- Learn product management fundamentals
- Understand market research and user research
- Develop data storytelling skills

**Phase 2: Transition** (6-12 months)
- Product strategy and roadmap planning
- Stakeholder management
- Cross-functional collaboration

**Phase 3: Mastery** (12-18 months)
- Product leadership
- Business impact measurement
- Team management

## Milestone Setting

### SMART Goals Framework

- **Specific**: Clear and unambiguous
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable indicators
- **Achievable**: Realistic and reachable
- **Relevant**: Aligned with career objectives
- **Time-bound**: Specific deadline

### Example Milestones

**6-month milestone**:
- Complete 2 online courses
- Apply for 5 jobs at target level
- Build 1 portfolio project

**12-month milestone**:
- Reach target seniority level
- Lead 1 major project
- Expand professional network by 20%

## Best Practices

1. **Start Small**: Focus on 1-2 high-priority skills first
2. **Be Consistent**: Regular learning beats intensive cramming
3. **Apply Learning**: Build projects using new skills immediately
4. **Seek Feedback**: Get input from mentors and peers
5. **Stay Flexible**: Adjust plans based on progress and opportunities
6. **Network Actively**: Build relationships across the industry
7. **Keep Learning**: Technology evolves, so must your skills

## Common Career Transitions

### Developer to Manager
- Develop leadership skills through project ownership
- Learn business and stakeholder management
- Practice delegation and team building
- Understand strategy and roadmapping

### Individual Contributor to Architect
- Deepen system design knowledge
- Learn scalability patterns
- Study real-world case studies
- Participate in architecture reviews

### Generalist to Specialist
- Identify your passion and strengths
- Deep dive into specific domain
- Build portfolio of specialized projects
- Become a thought leader in that area

Remember: Career planning is an iterative process. Your path will evolve as you grow, industries change, and new opportunities emerge. This skill helps you navigate that journey with confidence and clarity.
