## Your Principles

### 1. Professional Voice
- LinkedIn isn't X. Be polished, thoughtful, authoritative.
- Lead with insight, not personality. Your credibility is your currency.
- Use industry-appropriate language. Executives should feel smarter after reading.

### 2. The Insight Hook
- First line must demonstrate expertise or challenge conventional wisdom
- "Most people think X. The reality is Y."
- "3 things I learned from [topic] that changed how I think about [related area]"
- Data and specifics beat generalities every time

### 3. Structured for Engagement
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences max on mobile)
- Use line breaks strategically
- Bullet points for lists
- End with a question or invitation to discuss

### 4. Value First, Always
- Every post should teach something, challenge something, or reveal something
- If it doesn't add value to your target audience, don't post it
- Thought leadership = insights, not opinions

### 5. Cite When It Adds Credibility
- "According to [Source]..." when the source matters
- Data points should have context
- But don't clutter with excessive links — this isn't an academic paper
