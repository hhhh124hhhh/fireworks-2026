# Brand Style Transfer Templates

Curated examples from Nano Banana Pro case studies (Case IDs: 6, 7, 8, 9, 10)

## Template 1: Golden Abstract Style (Case ID: 6)

**Original Prompt:**
```
Nike Air Max sneaker rendered in golden abstract style. The shoe features flowing gold liquid metal texture with abstract geometric patterns. Clean gradient background. Dramatic lighting with golden highlights. Ultra-detailed 4K rendering.
```

**Customizable Parameters:**
- Brand Product: Nike Air Max sneaker
- Style: Golden abstract
- Texture: Liquid metal with geometric patterns
- Background: Clean gradient
- Lighting: Dramatic with golden highlights

**Variation Ideas:**
- Change to silver, copper, or bronze
- Use different geometric patterns
- Add brand logo as subtle watermark

### Studio Lighting
- **Clean, even**: Product-focused, no shadows
- **Soft, diffused**: Gentle, flattering

### Natural Lighting

## Troubleshooting Style Transfer

### Product Not Recognizable
- **Solution:** Add more specific product details
- **Example:** "Nike Air Max sneaker with visible Swoosh logo and Air bubble sole"

### Style Overpowering Product
- **Solution:** Emphasize product in prompt
- **Example:** "Nike Air Max sneaker, clearly visible, rendered in golden abstract style"

### Colors Wrong
- **Solution:** Specify exact brand colors
- **Example:** "Nike black and white color scheme, no other colors"

### Texture Too Subtle
- **Solution:** Add texture descriptors
- **Example:** "Visible embroidery stitches, fabric texture clearly shown"

### Background Distracting
- **Solution:** Keep background minimal
- **Example:** "Clean gradient background, no extra elements"

## API Configuration

```json
{
  "prompt": "[Brand product] rendered in [style] style...",
  "style_preset": "style_transfer",
  "parameters": {
    "style_strength": 0.8,
    "detail_preservation": 0.7,
    "color_preservation": 0.6
  },
  "quality": {
    "resolution": "4K",
    "detail_level": "ultra"
  }
}
```

**Parameters explained:**
- `style_strength`: How strongly the style is applied (0-1)
- `detail_preservation`: How much product detail to keep (0-1)
- `color_preservation`: How much to preserve original colors (0-1)
