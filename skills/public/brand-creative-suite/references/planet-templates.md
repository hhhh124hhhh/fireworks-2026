## Template 1: Realistic Brand Planet

**Original Prompt:**
```
A realistic planet themed around Coca-Cola. The planet's surface features the iconic Coca-Cola red with white swirling cloud formations. The Coca-Cola bottle shape is visible as geological formations. Golden hour lighting with atmospheric haze. Wide orbital view.
```

**Customizable Parameters:**
- Brand Name: Coca-Cola
- Brand Colors: Red and white
- Surface Features: Bottle shapes, cloud formations
- Lighting: Golden hour
- View: Orbital

**Variation Ideas:**
- Change to night view with city lights
- Add brand mascot or icon as surface feature
- Use seasonal themes (winter snow, summer atmosphere)

## Planet Type Guide

### Realistic Planets

**Characteristics:**
- Natural textures and geological features
- Atmospheric haze and clouds
- Realistic lighting and shadows

**Best For:**
- Established brands wanting grounded feel
- Corporate campaigns
- Global messaging

**Example Brands:**
- Coca-Cola, McDonald's, Nike, Google

---

### Stylized Planets

**Characteristics:**
- Geometric shapes and patterns
- Clean, graphic design aesthetic
- Bold color blocks

**Best For:**
- Tech brands
- Modern companies
- Brand identity campaigns

**Example Brands:**
- PlayStation, Xbox, Apple, Microsoft

## Surface Element Ideas

### Logo Integration
- Logo shape as continent or landmass
- Logo elements as mountains or valleys
- Logo colors forming bands or zones

### Product Shapes
- Bottles as mountain ranges
- Cans as geological formations
- Packaging shapes as surface features

### Brand Symbols
- Mascots as floating satellites
- Icons as surface patterns
- Symbols as cloud formations

### Brand Colors
- Gradient bands representing color palette
- Mosaic patterns using brand colors
- Color zones based on brand divisions

## Lighting Strategies

### Golden Hour
- Time: Sunrise/sunset
- Effect: Warm, nostalgic, premium
- Best For: Luxury brands, established products

### Bright Daylight
- Type: Full sun, blue sky
- Effect: Fresh, energetic, clear
- Best For: Lifestyle, sports, outdoor brands

### Night with City Lights
- Time: Night, artificial light
- Effect: Modern, urban, tech-forward
- Best For: Technology, automotive, urban brands

### Dramatic Space Lighting
- Type: Starlight, nebula background
- Effect: Cosmic, grand, epic
- Best For: Innovation, global messaging

### Soft Overcast
- Type: Diffused light, no harsh shadows
- Effect: Gentle, approachable, safe
- Best For: Healthcare, family products, everyday brands

## View Composition

### Wide Orbital View
- Shows entire planet from space
- Best For: Global messaging, brand overview
- Composition: Planet centered, starfield background

### Medium-Distance Shot
- Shows half planet, some surface detail
- Best For: Feature highlights, brand storytelling
- Composition: Planet curve, visible surface elements

### Surface Horizon Shot
- Shows planet surface with horizon
- Best For: Detailed surface features, landscape focus
- Composition: Horizon line, foreground elements

### Close-Up Surface Detail
- Shows specific surface features
- Best For: Product details, specific elements
- Composition: Zoomed in, shallow depth of field

## Multi-Planet Campaigns

### Storytelling Sequence

1. **Introduction**: Wide orbital view showing brand planet
2. **Feature Focus**: Surface detail shot showing specific product
3. **Brand Story**: Medium shot showing brand history as terrain
4. **Innovation**: Tech planet showing future vision
5. **Global Impact**: Multiple planets in solar system

### Product Line Planets
Create separate planets for different product categories:
- Main brand planet: Core brand identity
- Product A planet: Specific product features
- Product B planet: Another product line
- All planets share color scheme but have unique surface elements

### Seasonal Variations
Same planet with seasonal changes:
- Spring: Blooming flowers, green landscape
- Summer: Bright, vibrant atmosphere
- Autumn: Changing colors, falling leaves
- Winter: Snow, ice, festive lights

## Prompt Engineering Best Practices

### Parameter Order
1. Planet type/style
2. Brand name
3. Surface elements (brand colors, shapes, icons)
4. Atmosphere/lighting
5. Camera view/angle
6. Additional effects

### Negative Prompts
Avoid:
- Unwanted colors or styles
- Inappropriate elements
- Competitor branding

### Iteration Strategy
1. Start with basic prompt
2. Add brand colors
3. Incorporate brand elements
4. Refine lighting and atmosphere
5. Polish composition and details
